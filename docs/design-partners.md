# Design Partner Guide

ATOMiK design partner work starts with a real workload, not a generic platform
pitch.

## Who This Is For

Teams with:

- a real state-heavy workload
- an internal technical champion
- urgency around sync, rollback, replay, change detection, edge constraints, or
  adaptive execution
- willingness to share representative traces, architecture notes, or baseline
  measurements where possible

## Two Public Paths

### Evaluation Access

For technical founders, engineers, researchers, infrastructure teams, and early
evaluators.

Request limited evaluation access and receive technical updates, proof
artifacts, and availability for early demos.

### Design Partner / Paid Technical Evaluation

For teams with a real state-heavy workload, edge or embedded deployment,
distributed sync problem, or compute-efficiency requirement.

Work with ATOMiK to evaluate whether state-aware execution can improve a real
workload. Scope includes technical discovery, success criteria, prototype
mapping, and evaluation deliverables.

## Qualification Questions

- Where are you repeatedly moving or reconstructing state today?
- What is the most painful sync or replay path in your system?
- Where do you pay to rediscover what changed?
- What is your current rollback or state-recovery strategy?
- Which bandwidth, latency, power, or determinism constraints matter most?
- What would have to improve for a new execution model to be worth testing?

## Evaluation Deliverables

Depending on scope:

- workload-fit memo
- proof review
- benchmark or trace-exchange plan
- architecture mapping
- success criteria
- recommendation: continue, refine, or no-fit

## Evidence Hygiene

Live screenshots show current prototypes. Concept visuals show product direction
and are not represented as current shipped functionality. Performance claims
are only stated when backed by measured artifacts.
