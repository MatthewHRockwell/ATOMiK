# atomik.tech Site Architecture

This site is now organized around design-partner and customer evaluation first. Investors, licensing/IP partners, and technical evaluators remain important, but they are routed into secondary paths.

## Primary audience

Qualified edge and embedded teams that can provide one representative state-heavy workload, one current baseline, and one painful constraint expensive enough to evaluate.

## Primary CTA

Request Evaluation

## Secondary CTAs

- Review Proof
- Discuss Licensing
- Investor Diligence

## Page hierarchy

1. `/` - Homepage for customer prospects. It explains the painful constraint, what ATOMiK does, who it is for, what to bring, what ATOMiK evaluates, what the customer receives, what success looks like, what proof exists today, and the next step.
2. `/pricing` - Evaluation access. This is the clearest operational page: offer, intake package, metrics matrix, process, readout, success criteria, fit/no-fit, proof boundary, reservations, FAQ, and CTA.
3. `/benchmarks` - Proof packet. It explains evidence labels, claims registry, live measured artifacts, hardware validation, formal/software proof work, synthesis artifacts, roadmap/concept separation, and benchmark caveats.
4. `/solutions` - Use cases. It leads with edge/embedded and AI-at-the-edge evaluation paths, then routes remote, industrial, data-center, and licensing/IP use cases as secondary paths.
5. `/investor-brief` - Investor diligence. It positions ATOMiK as moving from proof to evaluated customer/IP opportunities and keeps investor claims evidence-bound.
6. `/contact` - Request capture. It asks for workload, current stack/baseline, painful constraint, timeline, and path.

## Claim policy

Public copy must separate measured, hardware-validated, software-validated, synthesis-validated, build-artifact, projected, conceptual, and roadmap claims. Cooling, battery, speed, bandwidth, water, power, footprint, and production-readiness language remains evaluation-target language unless a linked artifact supports the exact claim.
