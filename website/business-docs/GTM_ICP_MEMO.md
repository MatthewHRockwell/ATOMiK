# GTM / ICP Memo

## First ICP
Edge and embedded teams with one state-heavy workload, one current baseline, and one painful constraint around battery, power budget, heat, bandwidth, latency, footprint, weight, field reliability, or hardware overbuild.

## Why this ICP first
The pain is local, measurable, and tied to one constrained path. These teams can often provide traces, baselines, and correctness criteria without exposing the whole product.

## Deprioritized audiences
Investors, data centers, infrastructure partners, broad AI platforms, and generic compute buyers are important but secondary. They should be routed to diligence, strategic path, or proof-review flows rather than controlling the homepage narrative.

## Buyer titles
Founder/CEO, VP Engineering, Head of Embedded Systems, Director of Hardware, Product lead for edge systems, Robotics/IoT/industrial systems lead, Defense/remote systems technical lead.

## Evaluator titles
Firmware engineer, embedded systems engineer, hardware architect, FPGA/ASIC engineer, performance engineer, ML systems engineer for edge context, reliability engineer.

## Trigger events
Battery miss, thermal issue, bandwidth cost or radio duty-cycle pressure, field reliability problem, latency miss, hardware overbuild, feature scope reduction, expensive manual optimization, or inability to fit local intelligence into current device limits.

## Sales motion
Inbound proof review, qualification, intake call, customer evaluation package, workload map, baseline comparison, proof/evidence readout, fit/no-fit recommendation, scoped next step.

## Design partner motion
Move only after one agreed metric, baseline, correctness method, decision threshold, data access, and claim boundary are defined.

## Qualification criteria
One workload, one baseline, one constraint, one success metric, one correctness oracle, target environment, and an owner who can make or influence the next-step decision.
