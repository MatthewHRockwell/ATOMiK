## Title

ATOMIK: An Externally Stateless, Delta-Driven Computing Architecture for Deterministic State Evolution

## Filing Status

## High-Level Subject Matter

The filing describes a computing architecture and state-management approach using deterministic state transitions and delta representations. The investor-safe summary is that ATOMiK separates known reference state from accumulated meaningful change, then reconstructs state at defined boundaries when needed.

## What It May Cover

Potential coverage areas may include architecture-level treatment of reference state plus accumulated deltas, deterministic state evolution, state transition boundaries, and related implementation patterns. Final claim scope, patentability, and enforceability require counsel review and prosecution.

## What It Does Not Prove

- It does not prove production readiness.
- It does not prove customer workload value.
- It does not prove battery, heat, cooling, water, power-bill, or footprint savings.
- It does not prove that every implementation of state-aware compute is covered.
