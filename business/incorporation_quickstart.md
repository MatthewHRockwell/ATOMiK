# Delaware C-Corp Conversion — Fastest Path

**Current:** Rockwell Industries, LLC (California, since May 2023)
**Target:** Delaware C-Corp (required for SAFE notes, SBIR, YC, most investors)

---

## Option A: Stripe Atlas ($500) — RECOMMENDED
- **Time:** ~1 week to Delaware filing, 2-3 weeks to full setup
- **What you get:** Delaware C-Corp, EIN, Stripe business banking, cap table on Carta, legal docs
- **URL:** https://atlas.stripe.com
- **Process:** Online form → Stripe handles everything → You sign digitally
- **Note:** Stripe Atlas does NEW incorporations. For conversion from existing LLC, you may need to form a new C-Corp and assign IP from LLC → C-Corp

## Option B: Clerky ($500-800)
- **Time:** ~1 week
- **What you get:** Delaware C-Corp, standard YC-style docs, cap table
- **URL:** https://clerky.com
- **Better for:** If you want YC-standard legal docs out of the box

## Option C: DIY ($150-250) — CHEAPEST
- **Time:** 1-2 days to file, 1-2 weeks for Delaware to process
- **Steps:**
  1. Go to https://icis.corp.delaware.gov/ecorp/
  2. File Certificate of Incorporation ($89 filing fee)
  3. Get a registered agent in Delaware (~$50/year, e.g., Northwest Registered Agent)
  4. File Form SS-4 with IRS for new EIN (free, instant online)
  5. Draft bylaws, board consent, stock issuance, IP assignment (templates widely available)
- **Risk:** You're doing your own legal. Fine for bootstrapping, but you'll want a lawyer to clean up before a priced round.

## Option D: Doola ($197/year)
- **Time:** ~1 week
- **What you get:** Delaware C-Corp, EIN, registered agent, operating agreement
- **URL:** https://doola.com
- **Good middle ground** between DIY and Stripe Atlas

## CRITICAL: IP Assignment

Regardless of path, you MUST execute an **Invention Assignment Agreement** that transfers all ATOMiK IP (patent application, code, proofs, trade secrets) from Matthew H. Rockwell personally to the new C-Corp. Without this, investors will walk.

Template exists at: `business/data_room/02_legal/ip_assignment_template.md` (verify)

## CRITICAL: 83(b) Election

When you issue yourself founder shares, file an **83(b) election** with the IRS within 30 days. This prevents a massive tax bill later. Your accountant or Stripe Atlas/Clerky will handle this.
