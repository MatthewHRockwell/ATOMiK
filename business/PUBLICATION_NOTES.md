Before sharing any review-required material externally:

1. Check every performance, power, cost, customer, production, or traction claim
   against `../results/claims_registry.yaml`.
2. Add the correct evidence label from `../docs/evidence-labels.md`.
3. Replace any unsupported result language with `PROJECTED`, `CONCEPTUAL`, or
   `ROADMAP` wording.
4. Remove stale pricing/free-trial language and use request-based evaluation
   access unless a counsel-reviewed commercial offer supersedes it.
