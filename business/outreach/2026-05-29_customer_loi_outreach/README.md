# ATOMiK Customer / LOI Outreach Pack

Generated: 2026-05-29
Purpose: start real design-partner, IP/licensing, and LOI conversations without turning uncontacted targets into claimed pipeline.

## Files

- `TARGETS.csv` - ranked contacts and public contact routes.
- `EMAIL_DRAFTS.md` - target-specific first emails plus segment templates.
- `FOLLOW_UP_AND_LOI_SEQUENCE.md` - follow-up sequence and non-binding LOI ask after a positive call.
- `SEND_TRACKER.csv` - blank tracker for sent date, response, next step, and status.

## Use This Way

1. Start with Tier 1 targets. Send 8-12 messages first, not all 30 at once.
2. Prefer direct public emails where available; otherwise use the official company contact form and address the message to the named person/team.
3. Do not attach the full deck to the first cold email. Offer the one-pager or a two-page proof brief if they respond.
4. The first goal is a 20-minute discovery call. The second goal is a scoped evaluation. The LOI ask comes only after they acknowledge a real workload or route you to a workload owner.
5. Do not describe any target as pipeline, customer, design partner, LOI, or traction until there is a confirmed meeting, written interest, signed LOI, paid evaluation, or artifact.

## Claim Guardrails

Safe language:

- ATOMiK evaluates whether one constrained state path can reduce wasted state movement against the current baseline.
- The result may be fit or no-fit.
- Current proof is hardware-backed and artifact-bound.
- Battery, heat, power, runtime, bandwidth, footprint, and ROI outcomes require workload-specific measurement.

Avoid in cold outreach:

- guaranteed battery, heat, power, thermal, water, footprint, or cost savings
- universal speedup
- production readiness
- customer traction
- signed design partners
- broad replacement of CPUs, GPUs, NPUs, compression, caching, or sync protocols
