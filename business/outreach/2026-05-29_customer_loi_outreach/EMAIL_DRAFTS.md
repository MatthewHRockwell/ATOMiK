# ATOMiK Outreach Email Drafts

Use these as first-touch drafts. Replace bracketed text before sending. Keep the first email short. The goal is a conversation, not an LOI in the first sentence.

## Universal Rules

- No attachments on the first touch unless you already know them.
- Ask for 20 minutes or the correct technical owner.
- Mention LOI only after there is interest in a specific workload.
- If the recipient is a CEO/founder, make the ask strategic and short.
- If the recipient is sales/BD/contact-form route, ask to route to the owner of edge runtime, robotics data, embedded systems, or IP/product partnerships.
