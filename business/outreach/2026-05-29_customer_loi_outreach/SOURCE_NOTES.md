# Source Notes

This pack uses public company pages, official contact pages, public filings, and reputable third-party directories where official direct email was not available. Do not treat third-party directory email addresses as guaranteed decision-maker inboxes.

## Contact Rules Used

- Use public direct emails only where visible in public sources.
- If a direct person email is unavailable, use official contact/sales/technical-partnership forms and address the note to the named person/team.
- Do not guess private email patterns.
- Do not imply the target is active pipeline until they respond or a meeting is booked.

## Public Source Types

- Official company leadership/about pages for names and roles.
- Official contact/sales pages for company routes.
- Public filings or public PDFs for direct executive emails where available.
- Third-party directories only for supplemental route discovery, marked lower confidence in `TARGETS.csv`.
