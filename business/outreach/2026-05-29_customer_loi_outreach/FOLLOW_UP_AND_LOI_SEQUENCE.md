# Follow-Up And LOI Sequence

Use this only after the first email. Do not ask for an LOI before the target acknowledges a real workload or routes you to a workload owner.

## Follow-Up 1: 3-4 Days Later

Subject: Re: [original subject]

Hi [Name],

Bumping this once in case it was buried. I am not trying to sell a platform swap. I am looking for a quick fit/no-fit conversation around one constrained state path where [battery / heat / bandwidth / runtime / packet budget / replay / telemetry] already matters.

If this is the wrong person, who owns that kind of workload on your side?

Best,
Matthew

## Follow-Up 2: 7-10 Days Later

Subject: should I park this?

Hi [Name],

Last note from me. If the timing is wrong, I will park it.

If the topic is relevant, I can send a one-page summary first and you can decide whether a 20-minute conversation is worth it. The evaluation frame is simple: one workload, one baseline, one constraint, then a measured fit/no-fit readout.

Best,
Matthew

## Positive Response: Discovery Call Confirmation

Subject: ATOMiK fit call - [date/time]

Hi [Name],

Thanks. For the call, I will keep it focused around three questions:

1. What state-heavy path is already painful?
2. What baseline and success metric would matter?
3. If there is a fit, would the next step be a proof review, paid evaluation, design-partner conversation, or IP/licensing review?

I will avoid a generic deck walkthrough unless useful. The goal is to decide quickly whether ATOMiK is worth testing or whether it is no-fit for your environment.

Best,
Matthew

## After Good Call: Non-Binding LOI Ask

Subject: non-binding evaluation LOI for [workload]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for the conversation. Based on what we discussed, I think the useful next step is a lightweight, non-binding LOI around the specific evaluation path rather than a vague “stay in touch.”

The LOI would not commit [Company] to a purchase, production deployment, or IP transfer. It would simply document intent to explore whether ATOMiK is a fit for:

- Workload: [specific workload]
- Current baseline: [baseline]
- Constraint: [battery / heat / bandwidth / latency / runtime / packet budget / hardware limit]
- Success metric: [metric]
- Next step if fit: [paid evaluation / design partner SOW / IP review / technical diligence]

If you are open to that, I can send a one-page non-binding outline for counsel/business review.

Best,
Matthew
