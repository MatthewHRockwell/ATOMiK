**Subject:** quick follow-up on evaluation fit

Following up in case it helps to keep this lightweight. If you send one

### Email 3 - 7 days later

**Subject:** closing the loop for now

I am going to close the loop after this note. If the timing changes, send over
the workload and I will reply with either a fit opinion or a direct next step.
