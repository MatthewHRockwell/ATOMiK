# ATOMiK Deck Outline

1. **ATOMiK** - State-aware execution for systems that waste too much work
   rediscovering what changed. Visual: live screenshot crop.
2. **Problem** - Modern systems repeatedly move, replay, and rescan full state.
3. **Primitive** - State change becomes a first-class compute primitive.
4. **Customer consequence** - Lower state movement, cleaner rollback, simpler
   synchronization, more adaptive execution.
5. **Live proof today** - Current hardware-backed prototype, proof stack, and
   labeled artifacts.
6. **Initial wedge** - Edge, embedded, distributed, and sync-heavy workloads.
7. **Adoption path** - Software exploration, workload test, hardware-backed
   evaluation, broader integration.
8. **ATOMiK Desk + Resource Fabric** - Product direction, clearly labeled as
   concept / roadmap when appropriate.
9. **Business model** - Evaluations and design partners first; support,
   integration, targeted licensing, and enterprise paths later.
10. **Competition / alternatives** - Replace specific expensive status quo
    behaviors, not every processor or platform.
11. **Roadmap** - Live, validated, concept, and roadmap separated by evidence
    tier.
12. **Ask** - Design-partner workloads, technical advisors, investor
    conversations.
