# ATOMiK Slide Copy

## 1. ATOMiK

State-aware execution for systems that waste too much work rediscovering what
changed.

Evidence label: `HARDWARE_VALIDATED` live screenshot.

## 2. The Problem

Modern systems repeatedly move, replay, and rescan full state even when change
is sparse.

## 3. The Primitive

ATOMiK makes state change, delta application, and adaptive execution
first-class compute primitives.

## 4. Customer Consequence

Less unnecessary state movement. Cleaner rollback. Simpler synchronization.
More adaptive execution around what actually changed.

## 5. Live Proof Today

Current live hardware prototype, public software artifacts, formal proof work,
and recorded validation artifacts labeled by evidence tier.

## 6. Initial Wedge

Start where state movement pain is acute: edge, embedded, distributed, and
sync-heavy workloads.

## 7. Adoption Path

Explore in software. Test one real workload. Move into hardware-backed
evaluation where justified. Assess broader integration only after fit is clear.

## 8. ATOMiK Desk + Resource Fabric

Desk makes state-aware compute visible. Resource Fabric is the roadmap direction
for adaptive workload surfaces. Concept visuals are not current commercial UI.

## 9. Business Model

Near term: paid evaluations and design partners. Mid term: support,
integration, targeted licensing, and enterprise deployment paths.

## 10. Competition

The first competitor is the status quo: full-state movement, replay, rescans,
and expensive orchestration treated as normal.

## 11. Roadmap

Live proof, hardware validation, synthesis output, projections, concepts, and
roadmap are separated by evidence tier.

## 12. Ask

Bring one real workload, one technical champion, and one success criterion worth
testing.
