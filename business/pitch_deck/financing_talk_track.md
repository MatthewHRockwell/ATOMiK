## 15-Second Answer

We are raising a $5M target pre-seed. The round funds measured customer workload proof, IP/legal diligence, external ASIC feasibility, demo reliability, financial operating readiness, and design-partner evaluation. It does not fund tape-out.

## 45-Second Answer

The $5M target is a proof and commercialization round, not a production-silicon round. The target plan is $1.5M for engineering and demo hardening, $1.0M for customer proof, $750K for IP/legal, $750K for ASIC feasibility, and $1.0M for finance/GTM/ops plus reserve. The milestone is not a bigger claim. The milestone is measured customer workload proof: one workload, one baseline, one painful constraint, and a correctness-preserving readout.

## 90-Second Answer

Aggie has already seen the $5M ask, so use $5M target pre-seed in the room. The budget is tied to proof gates and commercial readiness. Engineering hardens the Zynq proof and measurement tooling. Customer proof funds workload baselines, evaluation SOWs, and measured artifacts. IP/legal funds patent conversion, prior-art review, entity/IP close work, and a diligence packet. ASIC feasibility funds external review before anyone talks about tape-out. Finance/GTM/ops funds CFO support, investor reporting, design-partner outreach, and operating readiness. Reserve protects the proof plan.

The financial discipline matters. ATOMiK is not asking investors to fund a generic hardware dream. ATOMiK is asking them to fund the evidence that tells us whether this becomes paid evaluations, design-partner traction, a licensing path, or a strategic partnership path. Strategic upside exists only if proof and IP diligence justify it.

## If Asked: Why $5M?

Because the next gate is not a simple demo. It is measured customer workload proof, legal/IP readiness, external ASIC feasibility, and enough operating capacity to pursue qualified design-partner evaluations. It is larger than a minimal proof budget, but it still does not become a tape-out budget.

## If Asked: What Valuation Cap?

We are planning a SAFE, but the structure and cap need CFO and counsel approval. Do not invent a cap in the room. Say the final cap, discount, pro-rata rights, option-pool mechanics, side letters, staged close mechanics, and closing details should be finalized with counsel/CFO.

## If Asked: Does This Fund Tape-Out?

No. This round funds ASIC feasibility, not tape-out. First we prove workload value, then complete an external feasibility review, then decide whether custom silicon is justified.

## If Asked: What Does Success Look Like In 18-24 Months?

A successful round produces:

- one or more measured customer workload artifacts;
- a counsel-reviewed IP packet;
- an ASIC feasibility go/no-go report;
- design-partner or paid-evaluation traction strong enough to support the next financing or strategic partner conversation;
- licensing-ready diligence materials if proof supports that path.
