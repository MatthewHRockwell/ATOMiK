# Financing Talk Track - Aggie Angel Pitch

> Current prep draft: 2026-05-23. Use this for pitch practice. Final financing
> terms require fractional CFO and counsel approval.

## 15-Second Answer

We are raising a $2.0M target pre-seed, with a $1.25M minimum viable close and a
$2.75M stretch plan. The round funds measured customer proof, IP strengthening,
ASIC feasibility, demo reliability, and CFO-led financing operations. It does
not fund tape-out.

## 45-Second Answer

We are raising a $2.0M target pre-seed. The use of funds is deliberately
proof-gated: $600K for engineering and demo hardening, $400K for measured
customer workload proof, $300K for IP and legal, $300K for ASIC feasibility,
$250K for finance/GTM/ops, and $150K reserve. The milestone is not another
abstract demo. The milestone is measured customer-value proof: a workload where
ATOMiK can be measured against bytes moved, latency, bandwidth, power/thermal proxy, or footprint
pressure that a customer already cares about.

## 90-Second Answer

The ask is a $2.0M target pre-seed. We also have a $1.25M minimum viable close
and a $2.75M stretch plan, so the round can flex without changing the strategy.

The budget is tied to proof gates. About 30% goes to engineering and demo
hardening so the Zynq proof is repeatable and partner-ready. About 20% goes to
customer workload proof: baseline harnesses, evaluation SOWs, and measured
artifacts around bytes moved, latency, bandwidth, power/thermal proxy, or footprint pressure. Another 15% goes
to IP and legal so the provisional position becomes a diligence-ready patent
packet. Another 15% goes to ASIC feasibility. That is not a tape-out; it is an
expert-reviewed go/no-go path so we do not spend like a chip company before the
customer value is measured. The rest funds CFO support, investor reporting,
design-partner outreach, operations, and reserve.

The financial discipline matters. We are not asking investors to fund a generic
hardware dream. We are asking them to fund the evidence that tells us whether
ATOMiK becomes a paid evaluation, an IP licensing path, a strategic partnership,
or a strategic acquisition target.

## If Asked: Why $2.0M?

Because it is large enough to fund 18 months of the proof gates investors will
ask for, but still below the Q1 2026 PitchBook/NVCA median U.S. VC seed deal
value. It is also meaningfully above accelerator checks because this is a
hardware/IP company with patent, measurement, and ASIC-feasibility work to do.
The number is sized around proof, not production silicon.

## If Asked: What Valuation Cap?

We are planning a post-money SAFE, but the cap needs CFO and counsel approval.
I do not want to invent a cap in the room. The way we are handling it is to set
the ask and use of funds clearly, then anchor the cap to current market data,
lead-check size, dilution, and the close structure. I can send the finalized
terms after the CFO pass.

## If Asked: Does This Fund Tape-Out?

No. This round funds ASIC feasibility, not tape-out. That is the responsible
path. First we prove workload value, then we complete a mentor-reviewed
feasibility study, then we decide whether custom silicon is justified.

## If Asked: What Does Success Look Like In 12-18 Months?

A successful round produces four things:

- measured customer-value proof on at least one workload;
- a counsel-reviewed IP packet;
- an ASIC feasibility go/no-go report;
- design-partner or paid-evaluation traction strong enough to support the next
  financing or strategic partner conversation.
