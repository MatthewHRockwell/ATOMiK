# Pre-Seed Financing Plan And Use Of Funds

> Current CFO-ready draft: 2026-05-27. This is the investor-facing working plan
> for the Aggie Angel pitch. Final instrument, valuation cap, discount, pro-rata
> rights, close mechanics, and legal wording require fractional CFO and counsel
> approval before signatures.

## Recommended Ask

| Plan | Amount | Runway | When to use |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Minimum viable close | $1.25M | ~12 months | Keeps the company alive long enough to finish IP conversion, harden the demo, and run the first measured workload evaluation. |
| Target raise | $2.0M | ~18 months | Recommended investor-facing ask. Funds customer proof, IP strengthening, ASIC feasibility, first technical capacity, and financing operations. |
| Stretch plan | $2.75M | ~18 months at accelerated spend | Adds faster technical hiring, deeper evaluation support, and more complete ASIC feasibility without funding tape-out; longer only if spend is not accelerated. |

Suggested default instrument: post-money SAFE. The valuation cap and discount
should be set by the fractional CFO after reviewing current cap table, dilution,
lead-investor appetite, and counsel feedback.

## Why $2.0M Is Defensible

The target is large enough to fund the next proof gates but still disciplined for
an early hardware/IP company:

- below the Q1 2026 U.S. VC seed median deal value reported by PitchBook/NVCA;
- materially above accelerator checks, because ATOMiK needs legal/IP,
  measurement, and ASIC-feasibility work rather than a generic software sprint;
- sized around measured customer-value proof, not production silicon;
- leaves valuation discussion to the CFO instead of forcing an unsupported cap
  in the deck.

## 18-Month Milestone Plan

| Window | Milestone | Investor evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0-30 days | Lock financing structure, counsel plan, and design-partner target list. | CFO-approved use-of-funds model and counsel-reviewed IP checklist. |
| 30-60 days | Harden Zynq demo into repeatable investor/design-partner package. | Demo checklist, current screenshots, and failure-mode notes. |
| 60-90 days | Define first customer workload and baseline measurement plan. | Evaluation SOW and one metric agreed before work starts. |
| 90-180 days | Produce first measured workload artifact. | Heat/power/bandwidth/latency artifact with evidence label. |
| 6-9 months | Convert artifact into paid evaluation, LOI, or no-fit decision. | Customer artifact, paid scope, or documented learning. |
| 9-12 months | Complete ASIC feasibility scope and IP diligence packet. | Mentor-reviewed feasibility report and counsel-reviewed IP memo. |
| 12-18 months | Prepare next financing or strategic partner process. | Measured proof, partner pipeline, IP packet, and next-round plan. |
