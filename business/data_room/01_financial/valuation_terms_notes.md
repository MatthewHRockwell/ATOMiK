# Valuation And Terms Notes

> Internal negotiation notes. Do not send as investor copy. Final terms require
> fractional CFO and counsel approval.

## Current Recommendation

- Ask amount: $2.0M target pre-seed.
- Minimum viable close: $1.25M.
- Stretch plan: $2.75M.
- Suggested default instrument: post-money SAFE.
- Valuation cap: do not improvise. Set after CFO review.
- Discount / pro-rata / MFN / side letter: counsel review required.

## Negotiation Principle

Do not let the valuation discussion outrun the proof. The strongest posture is:

> "The ask and use of funds are set. The cap should be finalized with the CFO
> around check size, close structure, current market data, and dilution. We will
> not pretend a valuation is precise before that pass."

## Benchmark Anchors

  median seed deal value of $3.0M.

## Cap Discussion Guardrails

| Investor pressure | Response |
|---|---|
| "Give us the cap now." | "The working ask is $2.0M. The cap is being set with CFO/counsel so we do not improvise terms. I can follow up with the finalized SAFE." |
| "Why not a priced round?" | "A SAFE keeps this round efficient while we convert proof into customer and IP diligence. A priced round can make sense after measured workload proof." |
| "Is $2.0M too much for pre-seed?" | "The number funds 18 months of specific proof gates. Minimum close is $1.25M; stretch is $2.75M. The plan can flex without changing the strategy." |
| "What if you cannot raise $2.0M?" | "The $1.25M plan funds the minimum viable path: IP conversion, demo hardening, one measured evaluation, lean ASIC scoping, and CFO-led next financing prep." |
| "What if you raise more?" | "The $2.75M stretch accelerates technical capacity and evaluation breadth. It still does not become a tape-out round." |

## Terms To Avoid Agreeing To In The Room

- participating preferred or enhanced downside rights;
- unusual control rights before a lead investor and counsel review;
- exclusive strategic rights before broader strategic review;
- valuation cap set without dilution modeling;
- claims that the round funds production silicon.
