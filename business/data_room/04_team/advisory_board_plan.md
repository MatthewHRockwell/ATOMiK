# Advisory Board And Fractional Support Plan

> Current diligence draft: 2026-05-23. This reflects the Aggie Angel Network
> pitch narrative: customer value first, then IP and ASIC feasibility.

## Why Now

ATOMiK is technically ambitious and currently founder-led. The immediate investor
confidence gap is not effort; it is independent validation around finance, ASIC
feasibility, and customer pull.

The next support layer should reduce three risks:

1. Financing and valuation structure.
2. ASIC feasibility and tape-out path credibility.
3. Customer-value translation for real workloads.

## Priority Support Roles

### 1. Fractional CFO / Financing Advisor

**Why:** The pitch should not lock in a round size, valuation, or instrument until
an experienced finance operator reviews the model.

**What they do:**

- Set the pre-seed ask, valuation range, and instrument recommendation.
- Convert use-of-funds into a fundable 12-18 month plan.
- Prepare investor-ready financial assumptions and dilution scenarios.
- Review acquisition / strategic-exit framing so it is credible, not speculative.

### 2. ASIC Mentor / Silicon Feasibility Advisor

**Why:** Investors will ask whether ATOMiK can move from FPGA proof toward silicon
without underestimating tape-out risk.

**What they do:**

- Review RTL architecture, timing assumptions, and synthesis evidence.
- Identify the right feasibility study scope before any tape-out commitment.
- Pressure-test area, power, verification, foundry, and EDA assumptions.
- Help translate proof artifacts into silicon-diligence language.

### 3. Customer / Industry Advisor

**Why:** The new narrative depends on customer outcomes: heat, battery,
bandwidth, latency, and smaller hardware profiles.

**What they do:**

- Help pick the first design-partner vertical.
- Introduce buyers with painful state-movement constraints.
- Define realistic evaluation metrics for each workload.
- Keep the pitch anchored in customer value rather than technical novelty.

## Advisor Compensation Guide

| Role | Typical structure | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fractional CFO | Cash retainer or success-based advisory agreement | Confirm with counsel before any transaction-based arrangement. |
| ASIC mentor | Cash stipend plus small advisory equity | Scope around feasibility review and investor diligence. |
| Customer advisor | Advisory equity or referral/advisory agreement | Tie to introductions and evaluation design. |
