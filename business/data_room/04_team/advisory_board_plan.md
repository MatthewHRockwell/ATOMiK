# Advisory Board Plan

*Target: 3 advisors, 0.25–0.5% equity each, 2-year vesting*

---

## Why Now

Chris Bolt flagged solo founder risk. An advisory board signals:
1. Domain validation from respected industry figures
2. Network access for customer introductions and hiring
3. Governance maturity beyond a single founder

---

## Target Profiles

### Advisor #1: Semiconductor / FPGA Veteran

**Why:** Validates the hardware architecture, opens foundry relationships, credibility with hardware-focused investors.

**Ideal profile:**
- 15+ years in FPGA or ASIC design, VP/Director level
- Current or former at AMD/Xilinx, Lattice, Intel PSG, or Cadence/Synopsys
- Experience with IP licensing models (ARM, CEVA, Imagination)
- Published or patented in reconfigurable computing

**What they do for us:**
- Review ATOMiK RTL architecture and synthesis methodology
- Introduce foundry contacts for ASIC feasibility study
- Validate technical claims for investor due diligence
- Help recruit Hire #1 (FPGA/ASIC engineer)

**Sourcing channels:**
- LinkedIn (Xilinx alumni, "FPGA architect" or "IP design" in title)
- IEEE/ACM FPGA conference network
- Lattice / AMD investor relations → board member introductions
- AngelList — semiconductor advisors

---

### Advisor #2: HFT / Quantitative Finance Domain Expert

**Why:** HFT is the beachhead market. An advisor who has built or operated trading infrastructure provides customer credibility, market sizing validation, and warm introductions.

**Ideal profile:**
- Current or former infrastructure lead at a quantitative trading firm
- Deep understanding of tick-to-trade latency, market data processing, FPGA-in-finance
- Connections to Tier 2/3 firms (our initial targets, not Citadel/Jane Street)
- Willing to make introductions to infrastructure decision-makers

**What they do for us:**
- Validate ATOMiK's HFT value proposition (10.6 ns latency, instant rollback)
- Introduce 3–5 prospects for pilot conversations
- Review HFT vertical module design (tick processor, order book delta tracking)
- Provide realistic sales cycle estimates for IP licensing in finance

**Sourcing channels:**
- Trading firm alumni networks (Jump, Two Sigma, Hudson River, Virtu)
- FPGA-in-finance meetups and conferences (FPL, Hot Chips finance track)
- QuantConnect / Quantopian alumni community
- Chicago / NYC quant meetup circuit

---

### Advisor #3: IP Licensing / Semiconductor Business

**Why:** The ARM-model IP licensing strategy needs validation from someone who has done it. This advisor helps structure deals, set pricing, and navigate the IP licensing sales cycle.

**Ideal profile:**
- Business development or licensing executive from an IP company
- Experience structuring IP license agreements ($500K–$5M per design)
- Understanding of per-unit royalty models, NRE vs. recurring, and customer negotiation
- Connections to potential licensees (chip designers, OEMs, system integrators)

**What they do for us:**
- Review and validate pricing model ($500K–$5M per design + royalty)
- Help structure first 2–3 license agreements
- Provide realistic sales cycle data (9–18 months for semiconductor IP)
- Introduce potential licensees from their network
- Validate revenue projections for investors

**Sourcing channels:**
- ARM alumni (business development, licensing teams)
- CEVA, Imagination Technologies, Rambus alumni
- Semiconductor IP industry conferences (DAC, DesignCon)
- IP licensing law firms (they know who's active in the space)
