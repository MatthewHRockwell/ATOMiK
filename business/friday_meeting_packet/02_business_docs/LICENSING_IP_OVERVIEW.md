# Licensing/IP Overview

## What could be licensed
Potential licensing surfaces include architecture/IP, hardware primitive design, evaluation methodology, workload-specific integration patterns, and future embedded IP blocks. Final scope requires IP counsel and technical diligence.

## Potential licensing partners
Chip/IP partners, embedded systems companies, defense-adjacent hardware groups, industrial/robotics platforms, infrastructure partners, and strategic partners with state-heavy constrained workloads.

## Diligence materials available
- Provisional patent PDF in the controlled data room
- Product/technical overview
- Proof artifacts index
- Claims registry snapshot
- Evidence labels
- Financial and business plan materials

## What is not production-ready yet
No production ASIC, no universal workload proof, no guaranteed downstream battery/thermal/cooling/water/footprint outcomes, and no finalized commercial licensing terms.

## IP status
Use current legal data-room materials and counsel review as the source of truth. Do not state final IP ownership, assignment completeness, or patent strength without counsel approval.

## Partner architecture fit
Best fit where a partner has repeated state movement, sparse or repeated updates, sync/replay/reconstruction costs, or constrained local execution where correctness-preserving change tracking could matter.

## Next-step process
Start with proof review or technical evaluation, move to NDA if needed, map one workload, agree success criteria, and only then discuss design-partner or licensing diligence.
