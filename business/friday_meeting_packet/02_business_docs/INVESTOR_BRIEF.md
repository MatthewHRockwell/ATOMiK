# Investor Brief

## Investment thesis
ATOMiK may represent an architectural wedge in constrained compute: make meaningful change the unit of work, then evaluate whether reducing wasted state movement creates measurable value in specific workloads.

## Why now
Edge intelligence, remote systems, embedded AI, bandwidth limits, battery constraints, and thermal envelopes are making local state movement expensive enough to evaluate.

## Market wedge
Start with edge and embedded teams that can provide one workload, one baseline, and one painful constraint. Treat infrastructure and data center opportunities as strategic expansion paths after stronger workload proof.

## First ICP
Edge/embedded, AI-at-the-edge, remote/field systems, robotics/industrial/IoT, defense-adjacent, and hardware-constrained teams.

## Proof status
Evidence today is proof-bound: Linux userspace-to-FPGA validation, AX7020 matrix artifacts, Zynq demo artifact screenshots, formal proof foundation, and synthesis/build artifacts. Customer workload validation is the next gate.

## Business model
Proof review -> technical evaluation -> design partner -> licensing/IP diligence -> strategic partnership or embedded IP path. Planning revenue logic is paid evaluations first, then design-partner SOWs, then licensing only if measured proof and partner diligence support it.

## Use of funds
Current target plan in `business/friday_send/02_financial/ATOMiK_Financial_Static_Summary.md`: $5M target, with $1.5M engineering/demo hardening, $1.0M customer proof, $750K IP/legal, $750K ASIC feasibility, and $1.0M finance/GTM/ops + reserve. CFO/counsel review remains required before final terms. The static summary and workbook now include founder-prepared revenue planning ranges; they are not forecasts and should not be presented as booked revenue or committed pipeline.

## Milestones
SD boot and Zynq validation, first measured workload artifact, design-partner targets, ASIC/IP feasibility review, counsel-reviewed IP packet, and partner-ready data room.

## Risks
Customer validation pending, downstream outcome proof pending, ASIC economics pending, IP/corporate close prerequisites pending, and incumbent/status-quo alternatives remain credible.

## Ask
Capital, qualified workload introductions, design-partner intros, proof reviewers, ASIC/IP diligence help, and feedback on the first ICP and evaluation motion.
