# Design Partner Templates

These are controlled diligence templates, not signed customer evidence.

- `paid_evaluation_proposal.md`: draft scope language for a paid technical evaluation.
- `design_partner_memo.md`: draft design-partner evaluation memo structure.
- `loi_outline.md`: non-binding LOI outline for counsel review.

Use these only after workload, baseline, metric, ownership, confidentiality, and success criteria are agreed in writing.
