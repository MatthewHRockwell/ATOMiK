# ATOMiK Paid Technical Evaluation Proposal Template

This is an operational template, not legal advice. Commercial and legal terms
should be reviewed by counsel before use.

## Objective

Evaluate whether ATOMiK's state-aware execution model can improve a specific
state-heavy workload, sync path, rollback path, or edge/embedded deployment.

## Duration

Use one of these planning bands unless counsel/founder approves a custom scope.

| Path | Typical duration | Purpose | Fee treatment |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Proof review | 1-2 weeks | Review public/private proof against one workload hypothesis. | $750 reservation may be credited toward larger scoped work. |
| Technical evaluation scoping | 2-4 weeks | Define workload, baseline, metric, artifact plan, and fit/no-fit criteria. | $2,500 reservation may be credited toward written SOW. |
| Scoped technical evaluation | 4-8 weeks | Run a bounded evaluation against one workload and baseline. | Request-based fixed fee or milestone fee; do not quote until scope is written. |
| Design-partner evaluation | 8-12+ weeks | Deeper integration, benchmark exchange, or prototype path. | Custom SOW; may include partner-funded engineering. |

Final dates, fees, and deliverables must be confirmed in writing before work
begins.

## Partner Inputs

- representative workload traces or data
- state size and update sparsity
- current architecture diagram
- baseline metrics if available
- deployment constraints
- current rollback / recovery strategy

## ATOMiK Deliverables

- evaluation brief
- architecture-fit memo
- benchmark or artifact plan
- prototype mapping where appropriate
- final recommendation: continue, refine, or no-fit

## Success Criteria

Define 3-5 measurable or decision-quality criteria before work begins.

Required success terms:

- primary metric
- baseline and measurement method
- correctness oracle
- threshold for continuing
- evidence label required for any public or diligence claim
- decision supported by the result: no-fit, proof review complete, technical
  evaluation, design-partner path, licensing diligence, or investor diligence

## Fee and Payment Timing

Do not forecast revenue from this template. It is a scoping tool.

Planning structure:

- reservation fees are paid before review/scoping time is held;
- scoped evaluation fees should be paid by milestone or upfront, depending on
  procurement requirements;
- any reservation credit must be stated in the SOW;
- partner expenses, hardware, travel, test equipment, and third-party services
  should be explicit pass-through or included line items;
- final commercial terms require founder and counsel review.

## Out Of Scope

- promised performance outcomes
- production deployment commitments
- broad custom engineering beyond the scoped workload
- transfer of ATOMiK IP
- publication rights without explicit approval

## Confidentiality / Publication

For counsel review. Define who may publish screenshots, logs, architecture
notes, benchmark outputs, and partner names. Default posture:

- partner data and workload details are confidential;
- public claims require written approval and evidence labels;
- customer names, logos, quotes, and benchmark numbers require explicit approval;
- concept visuals are not proof artifacts.

## IP Handling

For counsel review. Clarify ownership of ATOMiK IP, partner data, workload-
specific adapters, and evaluation reports. Default negotiating posture:

- ATOMiK retains pre-existing architecture, IP, tools, proof methods, and
  evaluation framework;
- partner retains pre-existing product data, workload details, traces, and
  confidential architecture;
- new workload-specific adapters, benchmark harnesses, and reports require
  written ownership and license terms;
- no production license is granted by a proof review, reservation, or scoped
  evaluation unless explicitly stated.
