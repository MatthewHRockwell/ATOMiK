# Close Prerequisites Schedule

Last updated: 2026-05-27

Purpose: make counsel/CFO/legal readiness explicit before any institutional close. This is not legal advice; it is a diligence tracker for investor follow-up.

| Workstream | Current status | Owner | Target timing | Friday-safe answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Delaware C-Corp path | Current operating entity is Rockwell Industries, LLC; VC-ready Delaware C-Corp conversion or formation remains pending. | Counsel + founder | Before institutional close | Current entity exists; VC financing structure is counsel-pending. |
| Cap table setup | Internal founder ownership record exists; formal financing cap table remains pending. | Counsel/CFO + founder | Before term execution | Cap table will be finalized with counsel/CFO before close. |
| Founder IP assignment | Template exists, but final destination entity and assignment chain require counsel review. | Counsel + founder | Before close | Founder IP assignment is a close gate. |
| SAFE and side letters | SAFE structure expected; cap, discount, pro-rata, and side letters are not finalized. | Counsel/CFO | Before documents circulate | Ask/use of funds are set; final terms require counsel/CFO. |
| Option pool | Not finalized. | CFO/counsel | Before priced round or institutional close mechanics | Do not quote option-pool impact yet. |
| Prior obligations review | Advisor, contractor, grant, lien, and third-party rights review still required. | Counsel + founder | Before close | No unreviewed obligations should be represented as cleared. |
| IP/legal budget | $750K allocated in target plan; vendor/counsel quotes still needed. | CFO/counsel | Follow-up diligence | Budget is a planning allocation pending quotes. |

Do not say:
- Delaware C-Corp is complete.
- Final cap table is clean and complete.
- All IP is assigned to the final financing entity.
- SAFE cap or side-letter terms are finalized.

Say:
> ATOMiK has a current operating entity and is preparing a VC-ready corporate/IP package. Entity conversion, cap table, founder IP assignment, SAFE terms, and prior-obligation review are counsel/CFO close gates.
