should be reviewed by counsel before use.

## Success Criteria

Define 3-5 measurable or decision-quality criteria before work begins.

## Fee and Payment Timing

Fee, payment timing, conversion credits, and procurement terms are placeholders
for founder and counsel review.

## Out Of Scope

- promised performance outcomes
- production deployment commitments
- broad custom engineering beyond the scoped workload
- transfer of ATOMiK IP
- publication rights without explicit approval

## Confidentiality / Publication

notes, benchmark outputs, and partner names.

## IP Handling
