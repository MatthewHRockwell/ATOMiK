# ATOMiK Public FAQ

## Is ATOMiK available today?

There are live demos, public code, and hardware-backed proof artifacts available
today. Product surfaces and roadmap concepts are labeled separately.

## Do I need custom hardware to evaluate it?

Not necessarily. The right starting point may be software, a benchmark exchange,
or a hardware-backed demo depending on the workload.

## Is ATOMiK Desk the product?

ATOMiK Desk is the clearest expression of the long-term product direction. The
near-term customer wedge is state-aware execution for real workloads.

## Can I test ATOMiK against my workload?

Yes. That is the point of the evaluation path. Start with one workload, one
state-heavy bottleneck, and the evidence needed to decide whether the path is
worth deeper testing.

## Are concept visuals current product screenshots?

No. Concept visuals show product direction and are labeled as `CONCEPTUAL` or
`ROADMAP`. The current proof image is the live hardware screenshot.

## How are performance claims handled?

Measured results are labeled as `LIVE_MEASURED` or `HARDWARE_VALIDATED`.
Synthesis ceilings are labeled separately. Projected platform performance is
labeled separately again. Concept visuals are labeled as concept visuals.
