# Visual Asset Manifest

This manifest classifies the public-presence images used by ATOMiK public,
pitch, and investor materials. Use these captions verbatim or keep the same
meaning if space is constrained.

## Placement Rules

- Use live proof before concept art.
- Label every image in-page, in the README, and in pitch/business materials.
- Do not infer benchmark numbers, customer traction, uptime, performance, power,
  temperature, allocation percentages, water savings, battery life, or product
  maturity from concept images or UI screenshots.
- Visible numbers in concept visuals are illustrative unless separately backed
  by measured artifacts.
