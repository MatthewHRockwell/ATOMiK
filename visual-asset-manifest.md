# Visual Asset Manifest

This manifest classifies the six public-presence images provided for the ATOMiK
public update. Use these captions verbatim or keep the same meaning if space is
constrained.

## Placement Rules

- Use live proof before concept art.
- Label every image in-page, in the README, and in pitch/business materials.
- Do not infer benchmark numbers, customer traction, uptime, performance, power,
  temperature, allocation percentages, or product maturity from concept images.
- Visible numbers in concept visuals are illustrative unless separately backed
  by measured artifacts.
