# Replica Flow

## Status

**ROADMAP:** Replica Flow is the product and technical narrative around delta
propagation, sync surfaces, and state-aware replication. Public copy should pair
it with live proof rather than presenting the concept visual as a current
customer product.

## Why It Matters

Replica Flow is useful as a public story when the prospect has:

- sync-heavy distributed state
- repeated full-state copies
- replay-heavy reconstruction
- rollback or recovery overhead
- constrained bandwidth or edge deployment needs

## Public Claim Boundary

It is acceptable to say Replica Flow is a roadmap product surface for delta
propagation. Do not describe it as a deployed customer sync product unless that
claim has referenceable artifacts and explicit approval.
